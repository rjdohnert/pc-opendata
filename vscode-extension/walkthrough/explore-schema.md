## Explore your schema

Open the **PC/OpenData** view in the Activity Bar:

- **Database Snapshots & Files** — database files in your workspace.
- **Schema Explorer & Vectors** — tables, columns, and vector indexes in the active database.
- **Raft Consensus & Quorum** — cluster role and peer health when replication is configured.

Click the status bar item for storage-engine and buffer-pool telemetry (`SHOW ENGINE STATUS;`).
