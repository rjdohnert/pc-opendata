## Next steps

- **Import a spreadsheet**: in the integrated terminal run `opendata`, then `IMPORT WIZARD;` for a guided CSV import.
- **Protect your data**: `BACKUP;` writes a verified snapshot; `VERIFY BACKUP '<file>';` proves it restorable.
- **Share a server**: see the *Network Servers & Wire Protocols* section of the user guide for TLS setup and per-table `GRANT` privileges.
- **Full reference**: open `user_guide.md` in this repository, or type `HELP;` in the shell.
